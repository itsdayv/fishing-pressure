Fishing Pressure Table

Get pressure data for your fishing spot and generate a 0–24 hour pressure table.

1. Get Your Fishing Spot Coordinates

  1. Open Google Maps.
  2. Right-click the spot where you intend to fish.
  3. Click the latitude/longitude coordinates to copy them.
  4. Paste them into the box below.
  5. Click Get Pressure Data. The page will request the data directly from Open-Meteo.

Enter coordinates in the format: latitude, longitude

2. Pressure Data

Pressure data is requested automatically from Open-Meteo when you click Get Pressure Data.
