Fishing Pressure Table

Paste your weather JSON below and generate the 0–24 hour pressure table.

How to get your JSON:
  1. Go to the Open-Meteo URL below.
  2. Replace xx.xxxxxxxxxxx and -xx.xxxxxxxxxxxx with your latitude and longitude.
  3. Copy the raw JSON response from the page.
  4. Paste it into the box below and click Generate Table.
https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/gfs?latitude=xx.xxxxxxxxxxx&longitude=-xx.xxxxxxxxxxxx&hourly=pressure_msl&current=pressure_msl&timezone=America%2FNew_York&forecast_hours=25

Rules: Uses hourly.time[0] and hourly.pressure_msl[0] as TIME 0; ignores the current values; selects hourly indices 0, 2, 4 ... 24; converts hPa to inHg; displays pressure and change to 2 decimals.
